THE MONOLITH HOUSE

THE VISION

Designed as a dialogue between the raw coastal landscape and contemporary structural precision, The Monolith House stands as a testament to spatial purity. The design utilizes a palette of cast-in-place concrete, charred cedar, and floor-to-ceiling structural glass to dissolve the boundary between interior sanctuary and the expansive Pacific horizon.

Every angle was calculated to harness natural light, with the sun's trajectory creating a moving choreography of shadows throughout the day. The result is a living space that feels both grounded in its permanence and ephemeral in its atmosphere.
